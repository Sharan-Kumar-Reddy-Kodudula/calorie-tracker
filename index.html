Scan a nutrition label

Take or upload a photo of the nutrition panel. Photos are resized in your browser before upload. The app fills the food form below, and you check the values before saving. Needs an Anthropic API key (stored only in this browser; API usage is billed separately).

Add or edit a food

Enter values exactly as printed, choosing whether they are per 100 g or per serving. The app converts everything to per 100 g internally, so any quantity you log in grams or servings gets the right totals.

Saved foods

Recipes

Combine ingredients you've already saved. The recipe becomes a food with values per 100 g and a serving size equal to one portion, so you can log it like any other item.

Saved meals

Save any meal from the Daily Log as a template, then add it to another day in one click.

Monthly export

Download every logged item for a month as a CSV file. Excel, Google Sheets, and Numbers open it directly. Each row is one food, with its date, meal, quantity, and nutrients.

Import foods (CSV)

One food per line, in this order: category,name,kcal,protein,carbs,fat,satFat,fiber,sugar,sodium,servingG,servingLabel. Values are per 100 g or 100 ml. Leave a cell empty if unknown. Don't use commas inside names. Use a verified source such as USDA FoodData Central.

Backup

Your data lives in this browser only. Export regularly to keep a copy, and import the file to restore it or move to another device. The API key is not included in backups.
